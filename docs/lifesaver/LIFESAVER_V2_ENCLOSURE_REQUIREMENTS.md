# Lifesaver V2 Home Hub enclosure requirements

Prototype constraints for a later 3D-printed shell. Not a manufacturing release.

- Display opening with readable status and no glare trap
- Camera window plus a visible privacy indicator
- Microphone openings that can be physically covered
- Speaker vents
- Cooling path for a Pi-class board
- Board mounting bosses and standoffs
- Wire routing channels and strain relief
- Rotating-base attachment with limited travel and a stable center of gravity
- Service access and a removable rear panel
- Physical STOP button reach from the front/side
- Physical privacy control that is obvious when engaged
- Power connector clearance
- Prototype fastener pattern (M2/M3 TBD)

Do not claim this enclosure is certified, medical, or production-ready.
