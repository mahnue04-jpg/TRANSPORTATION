# Sample offer: 30-day Casting and Auditions sandbox pilot

**Demo / Early Access.** This is a sample offer for an independent producer or an online creator. It is not an invoice, not a signed agreement, and not a checkout link.

| Item | Sample terms |
| --- | --- |
| Price | $2,400 USD for 30 days |
| Production | One unpublished sandbox production |
| Roles | Up to 3 |
| People | Adults only. Minimum age 18 |
| Label | Demo / Early Access |

Harborlight, the names, and the audition links in the product demo are fictional. They are not customers.

## What the 30 days include

- A sandbox organization for an existing Nova account.
- Draft campaigns and up to three roles, with a deadline and eligibility questions. Campaigns stay unpublished.
- Consent, a retention window, and revocation. Revocation clears the contact details and the audition link from review.
- An https link for each audition. AMICOR does not store the video file in this pilot.
- A human review queue, shortlist, and rubric notes. Notes do not cast anyone. `automatic_cast` stays false.
- Email drafts that an organizer can approve or retry. Delivery stays off. Stop and unsubscribe suppression is recorded and nothing is sent.
- A timezone call sheet for the shortlisted role.
- A minimized CSV export for the organizer. Reviewers cannot export, and video links are kept out of the file.

## What the 30 days do not include

- A public audition page, paid ads, or distribution.
- Email, SMS, or WhatsApp delivery. No provider is included.
- Storage of raw applicant video.
- Applications from anyone under 18.
- A partnership or integration with Zeus, Netflix, YouTube, or any studio or streaming service.
- A claim about prior customers, revenue, or return on spend. None of those figures is offered here because none has been measured for this pilot.
- Production hosting. Turning the sandbox on requires a separate owner decision and a disposable or approved staging database.

## How a producer would see it

1. Sign in with an existing Nova account on a disposable staging process.
2. Open the casting sandbox and load the fictional Harborlight sample, or create one production with up to three roles.
3. Invite adult test participants to save a consent-based draft with an https audition link.
4. Review, shortlist, and preview a callback draft. Confirm the page still says nothing was sent.
5. At the end of the window, export the minimized list or revoke consent and confirm the contact data is cleared.

The browser preview at `/static/nova-creative/casting-pilot-demo.html` walks the same story without saving it. The sandbox organization is the copy that remains after reload.

## Next step

This sample does not start a pilot by itself. An owner has to choose a disposable database, turn on staging reads, and agree the written scope before any producer uses it. Public intake and real message delivery are separate decisions and are not part of this price.
