# Organize your inquiries and prepare your next follow ups

For small cleaning and home-service businesses with inquiries or unfinished quotes spread across a spreadsheet or exported list.

AMICOR can help organize the list, identify missing details and prepare follow-up drafts for your review.

## What you receive

- An organized inquiry list with duplicate source records identified.
- A tracker showing the recorded status, missing information and proposed next action.
- Follow-up drafts you can review and choose to send yourself.

## Starting option

Request a Free Scope Check. A supported Starter Task is $49 one time after the work, input size, deliverables and turnaround are agreed. Larger tasks and recurring work receive a separate scope. No purchase or subscription starts from viewing this offer.

For the scope check, describe your business type, roughly how many inquiries you need organized, where the list is stored and the result you want. Do not send customer records until the scope and transfer method are agreed.
