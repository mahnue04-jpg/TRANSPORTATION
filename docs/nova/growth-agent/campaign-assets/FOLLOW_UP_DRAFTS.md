# Sample follow up drafts

All records and amounts are fictional. These are drafts, not sent messages. The business must verify the quote, context, recipient, contact permission and timing before use.

## SYN 001

Hello [customer name], I am following up about your office-cleaning inquiry. Would you like to review the proposed scope or ask any questions before deciding? Please let us know what would be helpful.

Owner review: confirm whether the quote was actually sent. The two input rows share the same inquiry ID and are represented by one tracker entry; both source record IDs are preserved. No quote delivery or customer interest is inferred from that duplicate.
