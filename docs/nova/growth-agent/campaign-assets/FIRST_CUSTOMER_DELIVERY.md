# First inquiry-support customer: delivery procedure

Release status: prepared in source, not published. No live customer intake or payment has been verified for this campaign. The saved Ads Manager campaign remains a draft.

## Offer and boundaries

Free Scope Check; one-time $49 Starter Task only after written agreement. Do not accept a task just because someone clicked an ad. No automatic customer messaging, sales guarantee, subscription, or unlimited work.

## Receive and review

The new /nova-inquiry-support/ page links to the existing /early-access/ form with product=Nova Operations Agent and intent=scope. The server accepts consented business inquiries into AMICOR_LEADS KV when configured. Email notification is optional; inbox silence does not prove there was no form request. KV storage is separate from Growth Agent CRM; no automatic synchronization is implemented here.

Before public launch, confirm the authorized owner can retrieve one clearly marked synthetic request from the authoritative store. Use the existing owner-controlled Cloudflare account; do not share customer data or secrets in public source. Do not place a real payment or contact a prospect as part of this check.

For each real request, confirm business purpose, approximate input size, deadline, desired output, and that the customer is authorized to share the records. Start with a description, not a customer-list upload. Decline unsupported work or offer a separate scope.

Do not promise a deadline, correction allowance, or payment link that has not been agreed and verified. No paid work begins until the scope and payment arrangements are accepted. A scope request is not an order; an order is not proof of payment.

## Fulfill

Preserve the original input privately. Assign source IDs to preserve traceability. Flag potential duplicates rather than deleting uncertain records. Do not invent quote amounts, dates, consent, or customer status. Prepare drafts only for the agreed use; do not send them. Do not upload a real customer list into public GitHub or ad tooling.

Compare the tracker with the input: account for every source row, confirm duplicate flags and missing values, verify the agreed draft count, and review facts and tone. Deliver through the agreed private channel with a short note describing completed work and remaining questions.

Record customer acceptance and any agreed corrections. Record revenue only against a confirmed payment, with fees and refunds accounted for. Offer recurring support only after the first task is accepted and the customer wants it.
