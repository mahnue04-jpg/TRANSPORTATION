# Amicor Health ISF Overview

Amicor Health ISF is the first live operational module in the Amicor ecosystem.

## Mission in Practice

Coordinate healthcare transportation and access workflows for underserved and rural populations, with a priority on behavioral health continuity and reliability.

## Core Operational Scope

- Receive provider transportation requests
- Match requests to vetted independent drivers
- Coordinate pickups, status updates, and completions
- Provide visibility to providers, partners, and admins
- Track payouts and delivery outcomes

## MVP Focus

- Role-based user access for providers, drivers, and admins
- Transportation request intake and assignment workflow
- Notifications and scheduling coordination
- Completion confirmation and payout tracking
- Reporting basics for operational and grant visibility

## Non-Goals (Phase 1)

- Full enterprise claims processing
- Complex AI-based dispatch optimization in production
- Advanced multi-region infrastructure

## Strategic Fit

Health ISF proves the Amicor XS platform model and establishes the repeatable operating pattern for future ecosystem modules.
