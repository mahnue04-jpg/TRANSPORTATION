# Healthcare Access Model

## Problem Context

Rural and underserved populations often miss behavioral and primary care appointments due to transportation barriers.

## Access Model

- Providers submit structured transport requests
- Amicor coordinates vetted drivers for appointment fulfillment
- Status transparency is maintained for provider teams and operations
- Outcomes are captured for quality improvement and funding support

## Core Design Goals

- Increase appointment attendance
- Reduce avoidable no-shows
- Improve continuity of behavioral health care
- Support equitable access across low-density service areas
