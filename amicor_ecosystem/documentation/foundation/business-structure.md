# Business Structure

## Entity Model

- Amicor XS: core ecosystem platform and shared infrastructure layer
- Amicor Health ISF: healthcare transportation and access operations module

## Revenue and Funding Streams

- Service agreements with providers/partners
- Program-based contracts and pilot engagements
- Grants supporting rural and behavioral health access initiatives

## Operating Model

- Lean module teams with shared platform standards
- Partner-driven service deployment by region
- Contractor-based driver network with centralized governance
