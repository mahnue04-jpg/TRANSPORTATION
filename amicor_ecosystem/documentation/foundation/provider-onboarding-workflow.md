# Provider Onboarding Workflow

## Objective

Enable healthcare providers and partner organizations to begin submitting transportation requests quickly and safely.

## Steps

1. Partner qualification and service area alignment
2. Agreement execution and compliance acknowledgment
3. Portal account setup with role-based permissions
4. Request intake training and SLA orientation
5. Pilot request validation and go-live approval

## Required Artifacts

- Organization profile and contact matrix
- Service location and scheduling constraints
- Primary and backup operational contacts
- Escalation path for urgent ride exceptions
