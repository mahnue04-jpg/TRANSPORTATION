# Grant Strategy

## Strategic Aim

Secure mission-aligned funding to accelerate rural healthcare access coordination and expand service reliability.

## Priority Grant Themes

- Rural health access and equity
- Behavioral health continuity support
- Transportation barrier reduction
- Community health system innovation

## Execution Model

1. Build a grant calendar and ownership matrix.
2. Maintain reusable narrative and impact metrics library.
3. Align partner pilots with measurable outcomes.
4. Produce periodic grant performance summaries.
