# Partnership Outreach Strategy

## Goal

Develop a repeatable approach for acquiring and activating healthcare and community partners.

## Target Partner Categories

- Behavioral health clinics and networks
- Community health organizations
- Rural provider groups and referral coordinators
- Public health and social service collaborators

## Outreach Pipeline

1. Identify high-need regions and partner candidates.
2. Conduct discovery conversations and workflow mapping.
3. Define pilot scope, success criteria, and responsibilities.
4. Launch pilot and capture outcomes for expansion.

## Messaging Themes

- Reduced missed appointments
- Increased care continuity
- Transparent operational coordination
- Actionable reporting for leadership and funders
