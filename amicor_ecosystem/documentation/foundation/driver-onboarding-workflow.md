# Driver Onboarding Workflow

## Objective

Build a reliable, vetted independent contractor driver network for healthcare transportation execution.

## Steps

1. Application intake and identity verification
2. Background and eligibility checks
3. Vehicle and insurance validation
4. Service standards and platform orientation
5. Activation and first assignment readiness

## Ongoing Controls

- Periodic document re-verification
- Performance and reliability scoring
- Incident and safety escalation process
