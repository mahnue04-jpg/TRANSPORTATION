# Partner Workflow

This workflow explains how healthcare partners use and interact with Amicor Health ISF.

## Partner Journey

1. Partner organization is onboarded.
2. Staff receive role-based portal access.
3. Requests are submitted and tracked.
4. Status updates and exception notices are received.
5. Usage and outcome reports are reviewed.

## Partner Outcomes

- Better appointment adherence support
- Lower coordination burden on clinic staff
- Visibility into transportation fulfillment
- Structured data for quality and grant narratives
