About Amicor

Built to make medical transportation intelligent, human, and dependable

AMICOR HEALTH ISF LLC exists to connect patients, care providers, and professional drivers through a modern platform for non-emergency medical transportation.

Our mission

Deliver safe, timely, and coordinated non-emergency medical transportation that helps people keep critical appointments and return home with dignity.

Our vision

Become Minnesota’s most trusted AI-powered healthcare mobility network—where every trip is visible, every partner is supported, and every rider feels cared for.

Our values

Principles that guide every trip

Amicor’s culture is rooted in care quality, operational excellence, and responsible technology.

Care

We treat transportation as part of the care journey—respectful, accessible, and attentive to patient needs.

Reliability

On-time performance, clear communication, and accountable workflows are non-negotiable.

Partnership

Providers, drivers, and families succeed together when information and expectations stay aligned.

Integrity

We protect privacy, operate transparently, and hold ourselves to professional healthcare standards.

Innovation

Community

Minnesota communities deserve transportation that keeps people connected to the care they need.

Why Amicor was created

Medical transportation still runs on fragmented tools

Too many patients miss appointments because rides are hard to request, hard to track, and hard to coordinate across hospitals, clinics, dialysis centers, and drivers.

Amicor was created to replace that fragmentation with one intelligent platform: request intake, AI-assisted dispatch, driver mobile execution, provider partnership tools, and operational visibility in one place.

What makes Amicor different

  • Healthcare-focused NEMT workflows
  • AI-assisted assignment with human oversight
  • Dedicated rider, driver, and provider surfaces
  • Operations visibility for dispatch teams
