## The Problem We Address

Care teams often lose time and visibility when transportation is managed through scattered phone calls, texts, and informal follow-up. Common operational friction includes:

- Missed or delayed appointments tied to transportation logistics
- Unclear trip status after a ride is requested
- Recurring schedules (such as dialysis) that are hard to keep consistent
- Discharge and follow-up timing that depends on reliable non-emergency transport coordination
- Limited trip documentation for internal operational review

Amicor is designed to bring these workflows into one provider-ready coordination model.

---

## Value Propositions (Supported Framing)

### 1. Centralized ride coordination
Authorized staff can submit and manage transportation requests in one provider-ready workspace instead of fragmented calls and messages.

### 2. Clearer operational visibility
Staff can follow trip progress through the platform’s documented ride workflow so pickup and transport status are easier to monitor.

### 3. Structured request intake
A consistent request process helps care teams move faster on transportation logistics and reduces ambiguity about trip details.

### 4. Support for recurring care needs
Amicor can work with partners to coordinate repeating patterns such as dialysis and ongoing treatment schedules.

### 5. Trip documentation for follow-up
Completed trip workflow records support clearer operational and administrative review inside the partner organization.

### 6. Minnesota healthcare focus
Partnership conversations are oriented to Minnesota care settings and community access needs as Amicor prepares for launch.

## How Partnership Typically Works

1. Organization reviews Amicor at `/for-providers` and submits a provider interest form, or replies to outreach.
2. Amicor holds a consultation to understand transportation needs, workflows, and service geography.
3. If aligned, Amicor discusses partnership next steps, authorized users, and workspace access.
4. Authorized staff use `/app/providers` to participate in coordinated request workflows.
5. Teams monitor status and review completed trip documentation as rides move through the lifecycle.

The interest form creates a **partnership inquiry**. It does not automatically create a provider account.

---

## What Amicor Does Not Claim

The following statements are **not** part of Amicor’s approved partnership messaging unless separately documented and approved:

- Medicaid enrollment or reimbursement approval
- HIPAA certification
- Guaranteed insurance coverage for rides or riders
- Guaranteed transportation capacity or on-demand availability
- Confirmed government contracts

During consultation, Amicor can discuss operational fit, data-handling expectations, and partnership process without overstating regulatory or capacity status.
