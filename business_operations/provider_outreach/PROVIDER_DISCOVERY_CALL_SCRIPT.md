# Provider Discovery Call Script

**Amicor Health ISF LLC · Minnesota**  
**Typical duration:** 25–30 minutes  
**Goal:** Understand transportation coordination needs, assess fit, and define a clear next step—without unsupported claims

---

## Before the Call

- Confirm contact: `{{first_name}}` at `{{organization_name}}`
- Note organization type (hospital, clinic, dialysis, behavioral health, AL, SNF, rehab, county/community)
- Have ready: `/for-providers` overview points, intake checklist, and optional `{{meeting_link}}` for a follow-up working session
- Review claim guardrails (below)

---

## Opening (2 minutes)

> “Thanks for taking the time, {{first_name}}. I’m with Amicor Health ISF LLC. Amicor is an AI-enabled non-emergency medical transportation coordination and operations platform preparing for Minnesota launch. My goal today is to understand how {{organization_name}} currently handles care-related transportation, then share how our provider coordination workflow works—only where it seems relevant. Does that agenda work for you?”

If yes, proceed. If they want a shorter call, ask for their top two transportation challenges first.

---

## Discovery Questions (12–15 minutes)

Ask naturally; capture answers in the intake checklist.

### Current state and volume

1. How does {{organization_name}} request transportation for patients or residents today?
2. Roughly how many care-related rides do you coordinate in a typical week or month?
3. Which trip types are most common—appointments, dialysis, behavioral health, discharge, recurring treatment, or something else?
4. What happens when a ride falls through or arrives late?

### Roles and workflow

5. Who usually initiates requests—front desk, care management, nursing, social work, or another team?
6. How do staff currently learn whether a rider was picked up or arrived?
7. Where do transportation issues create the most staff burden?

### Access and geography

8. What cities, counties, or Minnesota regions do you primarily serve?
9. What days and hours matter most for transportation support?
10. Are wheelchair-accessible or higher-assistance trips part of your needs? (Capture as needs discussion—not a capacity guarantee.)

### Success criteria and decision process

11. If transportation coordination improved, what would “better” look like for your team in 60–90 days?
12. Who else should be involved in evaluating a partnership (operations, clinical leadership, administration)?
13. Are there reporting or documentation expectations we should understand for internal review?
14. What timeline are you working toward, if any?

### Closing discovery prompt

15. Is there anything about transportation access at {{organization_name}} that we have not covered but matters to you?

---

## Careful Amicor Overview (6–8 minutes)

Use only after discovery. Mirror what they said.

> “Based on what you shared, here’s the relevant part of how Amicor works. Authorized provider staff can submit or schedule transportation requests through a provider coordination workflow. Amicor’s operations and platform tools help coordinate an available qualified driver when one can be matched. Staff can monitor trip progress through the documented workflow and review completed trip records for operational follow-up.”

### Points you may use

- Centralized request handling instead of scattered calls and messages
- Status visibility through provider/operations workspaces for authorized users
- Support for discussing recurring patterns such as dialysis or ongoing treatment
- Partnership starts with consultation; workspace access is provisioned for authorized partners
- Public page for overview and interest form: `/for-providers`
- Authorized workspace path: `/app/providers`

### Points you must not use

Do **not** say Amicor has:

- Medicaid enrollment or reimbursement approval
- HIPAA certification
- Guaranteed insurance coverage
- Guaranteed capacity or that every request will always be filled
- Confirmed government contracts

### Safe answers to common questions

| Prospect question | Careful response |
|-------------------|------------------|
| “Are you Medicaid approved?” | “We’re not representing Medicaid enrollment or reimbursement approval in this conversation. We can discuss your coordination needs and partnership process, and any payer-related topics would need separate, documented review.” |
| “Are you HIPAA certified?” | “We don’t claim HIPAA certification. We can discuss data-handling expectations and whether a BAA or similar agreement is appropriate as part of partnership review.” |
| “Can you guarantee coverage / capacity?” | “We don’t guarantee capacity. Availability depends on operational readiness, geography, timing, and driver matching for each request. We discuss fit and expectations openly during consultation.” |
| “Do you have government contracts?” | “We’re not claiming confirmed government contracts. We partner with healthcare and community organizations through a consultation and onboarding process.” |
| “When can we start?” | “Next step is alignment on needs and partnership process. If there’s a fit, we discuss authorized users, workspace access, and coordinated request readiness. I can also send {{provider_link}} and share {{meeting_link}} for a working session.” |

---

## Close (3–5 minutes)

### If there is interest

> “Thank you, {{first_name}}. Based on today’s conversation, I recommend a short follow-up to map your request workflow and authorized contacts. I’ll send a summary to {{amicor_email}} for our records and share next-step materials. Would {{meeting_link}} work for scheduling, or do you prefer email?”

Confirm:

- Best contact method
- Additional stakeholders to include
- Any materials they want before the next meeting

### If timing is uncertain

> “Understood. I’ll send a brief overview pointing to {{provider_link}} so {{organization_name}} can review when ready. You’re welcome to reply to {{amicor_email}} whenever a consultation would be useful.”

### If not a fit

> “Thank you for the clarity. Amicor focuses on non-emergency medical and community care transportation coordination. If that need changes for {{organization_name}}, we’re glad to reconnect.”

## Tone Guidelines

- Professional, Minnesota healthcare-appropriate, and specific
- Listen first; do not pitch over their workflow
- Prefer “coordinate,” “help match,” and “discuss fit” over absolute promises
- For clinical emergencies, reinforce: call 911; Amicor is non-emergency only
