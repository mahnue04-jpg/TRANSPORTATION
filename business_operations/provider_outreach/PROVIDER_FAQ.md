**Amicor Health ISF LLC · Minnesota**  
**Alignment:** Matches and extends the public provider FAQ on `/for-providers`  
**Audience:** Internal teams answering provider questions; language is suitable for careful external use

## Public FAQ (Aligned with `/for-providers`)

### What organizations can partner with Amicor?

Hospitals, clinics, behavioral health providers, assisted living facilities, skilled nursing facilities, dialysis centers, and county or community organizations can request a provider partnership conversation. Rehab programs and related care settings may also inquire when transportation coordination supports care access.

### What types of transportation can be coordinated?

Amicor coordinates non-emergency medical and community care transportation such as:

- Medical appointments
- Behavioral health visits
- Dialysis transportation
- Hospital discharge transportation
- Senior and disability transportation
- Recurring treatment rides

Amicor does **not** coordinate emergency transport. For clinical emergencies, call 911.

### Can providers request recurring rides?

Yes. Providers can work with Amicor to coordinate recurring transportation needs such as dialysis and other ongoing treatment schedules. Recurring patterns are planned collaboratively; they are not a guarantee of unlimited capacity.

### How can staff monitor trip progress?

Authorized staff can use Amicor’s provider and operations workspaces to follow ride status through the documented trip workflow. Public overview: `/for-providers`. Authorized workspace: `/app/providers`.

### How do we begin a partnership?

Submit the provider interest form on `/for-providers` to request a consultation, or reply to outreach and schedule via `{{meeting_link}}`. An Amicor team member will follow up at `{{amicor_email}}` to discuss `{{organization_name}}` transportation coordination needs. The interest form creates a partnership inquiry—it does not automatically create a provider account.

## Additional Partnership Questions

### What is Amicor?

Amicor Health ISF LLC is an AI-enabled non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) coordination and operations platform preparing for Minnesota launch. Amicor helps healthcare and community organizations submit requests, coordinate available qualified drivers through operations and platform tools, monitor trip progress, and review completed trip records.

### Is the provider interest form the same as account creation?

No. The form on `/for-providers` is a consultation request. Workspace access at `/app/providers` is provisioned for authorized partners after partnership alignment.

### Who should attend the consultation?

Typically the person who owns transportation logistics day to day, plus anyone who would approve partnership next steps (operations, care management, administration). `{{first_name}}` can invite additional stakeholders as needed.

### How long does onboarding take?

Timing depends on scope, authorized-user readiness, and operational alignment. Amicor will outline expected next steps during consultation rather than promising a fixed go-live date in outreach.

### Can Amicor integrate with our EHR or existing broker?

Integration and coexistence topics can be discussed during consultation. Do not promise specific integrations unless documented and approved for that partner.

### What information is needed to submit a useful ride request?

Clear trip details generally include pickup and drop-off information, timing or appointment needs, rider assistance considerations that are appropriate to share, and an authorized requestor contact. Exact request standards are confirmed during onboarding.

### Will every request be filled?

No guarantee is offered. Amicor works to coordinate an available qualified driver when matching is possible. Availability depends on geography, timing, operational readiness, and driver matching for each request.

## Topics to Handle Carefully (Not Documented — Do Not Claim)

Use these answers. Do **not** invent approvals, certifications, guarantees, or contracts.

### Are you enrolled in Medicaid / approved for Medicaid reimbursement?

Amicor does **not** claim Medicaid enrollment or reimbursement approval in provider outreach or FAQ responses. If a prospect asks, say:

> “We’re not representing Medicaid enrollment or reimbursement approval. We can discuss your transportation coordination needs and partnership process. Any payer-specific topics would require separate, documented review.”

### Are you HIPAA certified?

Amicor does **not** claim HIPAA certification. Preferred response:

> “We don’t claim HIPAA certification. We can discuss data-handling expectations and whether a Business Associate Agreement or similar agreement is appropriate as part of partnership review.”

### Do you guarantee insurance coverage?

No. Do not state that insurance coverage is guaranteed for rides, riders, or partners. Preferred response:

> “We don’t guarantee insurance coverage in outreach materials. Insurance and risk topics, if relevant to a partnership, are reviewed through approved internal processes—not assumed in marketing claims.”

### Can you guarantee transportation capacity?

No. Preferred response:

> “We don’t guarantee capacity. We discuss service geography, timing, and operational fit openly, and coordinate available qualified drivers when matching is possible.”

### Do you have government contracts?

Amicor does **not** claim confirmed government contracts. Preferred response:

> “We’re not claiming confirmed government contracts. We work with healthcare and community organizations through consultation and partnership onboarding.”
