## G. Minimum Fields Before Moving to Onboarding Discussion

Complete at least:

- [ ] Organization name and type
- [ ] Primary contact name + work email
- [ ] Service area in Minnesota
- [ ] Transportation needs summary (trip types + rough volume or “unsure”)
- [ ] Current request workflow owner
- [ ] Agreed next step and date
- [ ] Confirmation that no unsupported claims were made in writing
