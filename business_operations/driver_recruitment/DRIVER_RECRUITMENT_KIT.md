# Driver Recruitment Kit

**Company:** AMICOR HEALTH ISF LLC  
**Service focus:** Minnesota non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) and related community transportation coordination  
**Audience:** Internal recruitment, operations, and onboarding teams  
**Purpose:** Provide a single, professional package for recruiting, screening, and preparing drivers to support Minnesota riders who need reliable access to healthcare and essential services.

---

## What this kit is for

This kit helps Amicor teams present driver opportunities clearly, welcome community-minded applicants, and move candidates through a consistent path from first interest to activation—without overpromising income, employment benefits, or guaranteed trip volume.

Use these materials when:

- Recruiting drivers in Minnesota communities
- Responding to driver inquiries
- Preparing interview and review conversations
- Guiding applicants through documentation and onboarding
- Aligning public messaging with internal process language

## End-to-end stages

Amicor’s driver pathway has five stages. Keep these distinctions clear in every conversation, email, and interview.

### 1. Recruitment

Attract and inform interested individuals about Amicor’s Minnesota transportation mission.

**Typical activities**

- Share `/for-drivers` content and community posts
- Acknowledge inquiries promptly
- Explain eligibility at a high level
- Invite qualified interest to apply

**Outcome:** Candidate understands the opportunity and is ready to submit an application (or asks clarifying questions).

### 2. Application

Candidate completes the Amicor driver application and uploads requested materials.

**Typical activities**

- Submit contact, vehicle, and experience details via `/platform-ops/driver-apply`
- Upload license, insurance, registration, and other requested documents
- Respond to follow-up or document reminders

**Outcome:** Application package is complete enough for screening and review.

### 3. Conditional approval

Amicor determines that the candidate may proceed to onboarding, subject to remaining requirements.

**Typical activities**

- Eligibility and documentation review
- Background / screening coordination as required
- Interview or readiness conversation when used
- Clear communication of outstanding items

**Outcome:** Candidate is conditionally approved to continue—not yet activated for trips.

> Conditional approval is not a guarantee of activation, trip volume, or earnings.

### 4. Onboarding

Complete remaining credentials, agreements, orientation, and platform readiness steps.

**Typical activities**

- Finish document and consent requirements
- Complete orientation on Driver Mobile and trip workflow expectations
- Confirm professional assistance readiness and service-type suitability
- Address any service-specific requirements (for example, ambulatory vs. specialized transport)

**Outcome:** Candidate meets Amicor’s operational readiness criteria for the relevant service type.

### 5. Activation

Driver becomes eligible for approved trip opportunities through Amicor’s coordination tools.

**Typical activities**

- Final readiness confirmation by operations / compliance as applicable
- Access to Driver Mobile for assigned opportunities
- Ongoing adherence to platform and service expectations

**Outcome:** Driver may receive approved transportation opportunities subject to availability, readiness, agreement terms, and applicable rules.

> Activation does not guarantee a minimum number of trips, schedule, or income level.

## Messaging principles

1. **Community first** — Emphasize helping Minnesota riders reach dialysis, appointments, discharges, and other essential services.
2. **No income guarantees** — Do not promise earnings, hourly rates, weekly minimums, or trip volume.
3. **No unconfirmed pay rates** — Do not invent or advertise specific pay figures unless formally approved for public use.
4. **No employment-benefit promises** — Do not imply W-2 benefits, paid leave, or similar employment packages unless expressly authorized in approved materials.
5. **Process honesty** — Distinguish recruitment, conditional approval, onboarding, and activation.
6. **Eligibility is review-based** — Final eligibility depends on Amicor review, screening outcomes, documentation, and applicable service rules.
7. **Do not invent contact details** — Use only approved placeholders or confirmed contact information from official Amicor sources.

## Suggested team workflow

1. Share `/for-drivers` for initial interest.
2. Direct ready candidates to `/platform-ops/driver-apply`.
3. Use inquiry / follow-up / document reminder templates as needed.
4. Complete interview scorecard when an interview is conducted.
5. Track document completeness with the onboarding checklist.
6. Communicate conditional approval only when review supports it.
7. Activate only after onboarding and readiness requirements are met.
