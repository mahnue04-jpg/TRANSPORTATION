## Suggested interview prompts (optional)

1. Why are you interested in non-emergency medical / community transportation with Amicor?
2. Describe how you would support a rider who is anxious about getting to an appointment.
3. How do you keep a vehicle ready for passenger transport (cleanliness, fuel, documents)?
4. Tell us about a time you had to communicate a delay or change professionally.
5. What does reliability mean to you when riders depend on transportation for healthcare?
6. Are you comfortable using a smartphone app for trip details and status updates?
7. Do you understand that approval depends on review/screening and that trip volume and income are not guaranteed?

## Reminders for interviewers

- Do not promise activation, trip volume, or income.
- Do not quote unconfirmed pay rates.
- Do not invent contact phone/email for follow-up—use approved Amicor channels and templates.
- Conditional approval and activation remain separate decisions after documentation and screening review.
- Direct candidates to `/for-drivers` for overview and `/platform-ops/driver-apply` for application materials.
