# Driver Application Process

**Company:** AMICOR HEALTH ISF LLC  
**Aligned with:** Public driver onboarding steps on `/for-drivers`  
**Application route:** `/platform-ops/driver-apply`

---

## Purpose

This document explains the Amicor driver pathway from first interest through eligibility for approved trips. Use it to keep recruitment conversations, email language, and internal status updates consistent with the website.

---

## Stage definitions (use these terms)

| Stage | Meaning | What it is not |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Recruitment** | Attracting and informing interested candidates | Not an application; not approval |
| **Application** | Candidate submits information and uploads documents | Not conditional approval |
| **Conditional approval** | Amicor authorizes the candidate to continue onboarding pending remaining items | Not activation; not a guarantee of trips or income |
| **Onboarding** | Completing remaining credentials, consents, orientation, and readiness steps | Not the same as being live for trips |
| **Activation** | Driver becomes eligible for approved trip opportunities | Not a guarantee of trip volume or earnings |

Keep these distinctions clear with applicants and internal teams.

---

## Public process steps (website-aligned)

These five steps match Amicor’s public “From application to approved trips” section.

### Step 1 — Submit application

**Candidate action:** Complete the Amicor driver application with contact and vehicle details.

**Where:** `/platform-ops/driver-apply`

**Team action:** Acknowledge interest and confirm the application was received (see inquiry / follow-up email templates).

**Stage after completion:** Application in progress / submitted for review.

### Step 2 — Upload required documentation

**Candidate action:** Provide license, insurance, registration, and other requested documents.

**Team action:** Review completeness; send document reminders when items are missing or unclear.

**Stage note:** An application without required uploads typically cannot move into full screening review.

### Step 3 — Complete screening and review

**Candidate action:** Participate in required screening and respond to review questions.

**Team action:** Review eligibility, screening results, and application completeness. Conduct interview when used.

**Possible outcomes at this stage**

- Request more information or documents
- Hold pending screening / review
- Conditional approval to proceed to onboarding
- Decline (communicated professionally and accurately)

> Screening and review determine whether a candidate may continue. Completion of Step 1 or 2 alone is not approval.

### Step 4 — Receive platform orientation

**Candidate action:** Learn how to use Driver Mobile and follow trip workflow expectations.

**Team action:** Provide orientation covering trip information, pickup/transport/completion steps, professionalism, and platform readiness.

**Stage:** Onboarding (post–conditional approval, when applicable).

### Step 5 — Become eligible for approved trips

**Candidate action:** Maintain readiness and follow agreement / platform expectations.

**Team action:** Confirm activation readiness for the relevant service type.

**Stage:** Activation — driver may become eligible for assigned transportation opportunities.

> “Eligible for approved trips” means the driver may receive opportunities through Amicor’s coordination tools. It does **not** guarantee trip count, schedule, or income.

## How stages map to the five website steps

```text
Recruitment
    → interest via /for-drivers, community posts, or inquiry

Application
    → Step 1 Submit application
    → Step 2 Upload required documentation

Conditional approval
    → typically after Step 3 screening/review supports continuing

Onboarding
    → remaining requirements + Step 4 platform orientation

Activation
    → Step 5 eligible for approved trips
```

## Recommended internal status language

Prefer clear, non-promissory status labels:

| Status | Suggested meaning |
|--------|-------------------|
| Inquiry received | Interest noted; application not yet submitted |
| Application started | Candidate began `/platform-ops/driver-apply` |
| Documents pending | Uploads incomplete or clarification needed |
| In screening / review | Eligibility and screening under review |
| Interview scheduled | Conversation scheduled using interview invitation template |
| Conditional approval | May proceed to onboarding pending remaining items |
| Onboarding in progress | Orientation / remaining credentials underway |
| Activated | Eligible for approved trip opportunities |
| On hold | Progress paused pending information or review |
| Declined / not moving forward | Not advancing at this time |

## Candidate journey summary (external-facing language)

1. Learn about Amicor at `/for-drivers`.
2. Apply at `/platform-ops/driver-apply`.
3. Upload requested documentation.
4. Complete screening and review.
5. Complete platform orientation.
6. If approved and activated, use Driver Mobile (`/app/mobile`) for approved trip opportunities.

---

## What recruiters should emphasize

- Amicor supports Minnesota riders reaching healthcare and community services.
- Technology tools provide clear trip information and coordination support.
- Final terms are set in the driver agreement and applicable service rules.
- Opportunities depend on readiness, approval, and platform / operations workflows.
- Earnings visibility (when available in platform records) is not a promise of income level.

## What recruiters must avoid

- Guaranteed weekly income or trip minimums
- Unconfirmed pay rates
- Promising activation on a fixed date without review completion
- Treating conditional approval as full activation
- Inventing phone numbers, emails, or office addresses
