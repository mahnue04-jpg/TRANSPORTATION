# Driver Requirements Checklist

**Company:** AMICOR HEALTH ISF LLC  
**Focus:** Minnesota driver eligibility for Amicor-coordinated transportation opportunities  
**Use:** Recruitment conversations, pre-screen guidance, and applicant self-check before applying

---

## Important notices

- This checklist describes **basic expectations** commonly discussed during recruitment.
- **Final eligibility depends on Amicor review**, screening results, documentation completeness, service type, and applicable rules.
- Meeting these items does **not** guarantee approval, activation, trip volume, or income.
- Amicor does **not** guarantee earnings, pay rates, or a minimum number of trips.
- Final requirements may vary based on service type and applicable Amicor / partner rules.

## How to talk about each requirement

### Valid driver’s license

Applicants should hold a valid driver’s license appropriate for the vehicle they will operate. Expiration, status, and any restrictions are reviewed as part of the application process.

### Acceptable driving record

Amicor reviews driving history as part of eligibility. Recruiters should not promise that a particular record will pass; outcomes depend on screening standards and applicable rules.

### Reliable vehicle

Applicants need a reliable vehicle that is properly registered and insured. Final vehicle suitability depends on service type (for example, ambulatory transport versus specialized service) and Amicor requirements.

### Current vehicle registration

Current registration documentation is typically requested during application or onboarding. Expired registration generally delays progress.

### Required vehicle insurance

Active insurance for the vehicle used in Amicor trips is expected. Specific coverage expectations are confirmed during review and may vary by service type.

### Smartphone access

Drivers use Amicor’s technology-supported coordination tools. A smartphone capable of accessing Driver Mobile is part of readiness for approved trip opportunities.

### Background screening

Background screening is part of the Amicor driver review process before a driver becomes eligible for approved trips. Participation in required screening is expected.

### Ability to assist riders professionally

Amicor drivers support riders traveling to healthcare and community services. Professional communication, patience, confidentiality awareness, and respectful assistance are essential—regardless of vehicle type.

## Recruiter conversation script (brief)

> “Amicor looks for drivers who can help Minnesota riders reach essential appointments and services. To get started, you’ll typically need a valid license, an acceptable driving record, a reliable registered and insured vehicle, smartphone access, willingness to complete background screening, and the ability to assist riders professionally. Final eligibility depends on our review—submitting an application does not guarantee approval or trip assignments. You can learn more at `/for-drivers` and apply at `/platform-ops/driver-apply`.”

---

## What not to promise

Do **not** tell applicants that Amicor guarantees:

- A specific hourly, per-trip, or weekly pay rate (unless formally approved for disclosure)
- A minimum number of trips or hours
- Immediate activation after applying
- Employment benefits associated with traditional W-2 roles (unless expressly authorized)
- Approval based solely on self-reported checklist completion
