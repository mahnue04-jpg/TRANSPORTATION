## Usage notes

- Keep posts community-focused and professional.
- Do **not** promise guaranteed income, weekly pay, trip minimums, or employment benefits.
- Do **not** invent phone numbers, emails, or street addresses.
- Prefer CTA to `/for-drivers`. Use `/platform-ops/driver-apply` only when the audience is ready to apply.
- When posting on platforms that require a full URL, prepend the approved Amicor website origin to the path.
- Adapt lightly for Facebook, LinkedIn, Nextdoor, or community boards; keep the meaning unchanged.
