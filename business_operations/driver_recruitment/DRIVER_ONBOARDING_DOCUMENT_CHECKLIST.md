## Important notices

- Items below are **typically requested**. Exact requirements **may vary by service type** (for example, ambulatory NEMT versus specialized / STS-capable transport) and by applicable Amicor, partner, or regulatory rules.
- A complete upload package does **not** guarantee conditional approval, activation, trip volume, or income.
- Mark N/A when an item does not apply to the candidate’s service path.
- Do not invent contact phone/email on this form; use approved Amicor channels for follow-up.
