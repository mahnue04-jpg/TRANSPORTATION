## Public FAQ (website-aligned)

### What documents are required?

Typical documentation includes a valid driver’s license, current vehicle registration, required vehicle insurance, and other materials requested during onboarding review.

**Internal add-on:** Exact items may vary by service type. Use the onboarding document checklist to track what was requested for each candidate. Completing uploads does not guarantee approval or activation.

### What type of vehicle can I use?

You need a reliable vehicle that is properly registered and insured. Final vehicle suitability depends on service type and applicable Amicor requirements.

**Internal add-on:** Do not approve a vehicle type verbally during recruitment. Suitability is confirmed during review. Specialized equipment paths may have additional requirements.

### Is a background check required?

Yes. Background screening is part of the Amicor driver review process before a driver becomes eligible for approved trips.

**Internal add-on:** Screening participation is expected. Outcomes are reviewed against Amicor standards and applicable rules. Recruiters should not promise that a candidate will pass.

### How are trip opportunities assigned?

Approved drivers receive trip opportunities through Amicor’s technology-supported coordination tools, with assignment and readiness managed through the platform and operations workflows.

**Internal add-on:** Activation means eligibility for approved opportunities—not a guaranteed schedule or number of trips. Earnings, when visible in platform records for completed trips, are not a promise of future income.

### How do I begin the application?

Select **Start Driver Application** on `/for-drivers` (or go directly to `/platform-ops/driver-apply`) to open Amicor’s driver application route and submit your information.

**Internal add-on:** For early interest, share `/for-drivers` first so candidates see eligibility, process steps, and FAQ before applying.

## Additional internal FAQ (safe, non-guaranteed)

### Who is AMICOR HEALTH ISF LLC?

AMICOR HEALTH ISF LLC is a Minnesota-focused company coordinating transportation that helps riders reach healthcare and essential community services. Drivers participate through Amicor’s application, review, onboarding, and activation pathway.

### Where does Amicor operate?

Amicor’s driver messaging focuses on Minnesota communities and riders who need reliable access to care and services. Specific service coverage for any driver depends on operational readiness and approved opportunities.

### What is the difference between conditional approval and activation?

- **Conditional approval** means the candidate may continue onboarding pending remaining requirements.
- **Activation** means the driver may become eligible for approved trip opportunities.

Neither stage guarantees income or trip volume.

### Will I be an employee with benefits?

Driver participation is described publicly as an independent work environment, **subject to the final driver agreement and platform requirements**. Do not promise W-2 employment benefits unless expressly authorized in approved materials. Point candidates to the agreement process during onboarding for governing terms.

### How much will I earn?

Amicor does **not** guarantee income or specific pay rates in recruitment materials. Approved drivers may review earnings information through platform records associated with completed trips. Do not invent rates. If a formal rate schedule is later approved for disclosure, use only that authorized source.

### Are trips guaranteed after I apply?

No. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval. Conditional approval does not guarantee activation. Activation does not guarantee trip volume. Opportunities depend on review outcomes, readiness, agreement terms, and platform / operations workflows.

### What basic eligibility items should I mention?

Consistent with `/for-drivers`:

1. Valid driver’s license  
2. Acceptable driving record  
3. Reliable vehicle  
4. Current vehicle registration  
5. Required vehicle insurance  
6. Smartphone access  
7. Background screening  
8. Ability to assist riders professionally  

Remind candidates that final requirements may vary by service type and that eligibility depends on review.

### What are the five process steps?

1. Submit application  
2. Upload required documentation  
3. Complete screening and review  
4. Receive platform orientation  
5. Become eligible for approved trips  

### Do I need a smartphone?

Yes, smartphone access is part of basic eligibility because drivers use Amicor’s technology-supported trip coordination tools.

### Can I ask questions before applying?

Yes. Candidates may use `/contact?intent=driver` or follow approved Amicor inquiry channels. For overview content, send them to `/for-drivers`.

### Where do drivers log in after approval?

Approved drivers use Driver Mobile via `/app/mobile` when ready to work approved trips.

## Phrases to avoid

| Avoid saying | Prefer |
|--------------|--------|
| “You’ll make $X per week” | “Earnings depend on completed approved trips and are not guaranteed.” |
| “We guarantee hours” | “Trip opportunities depend on approval, readiness, and available assignments.” |
| “You’re hired” | “You’ve received conditional approval to continue onboarding” or “You’re activated for approved trip opportunities,” as accurate |
| “Benefits include…” (unconfirmed) | Describe only platform-supported opportunities and agreement-governed terms |
| Unverified phone/email | Use approved contact placeholders or `/contact?intent=driver` |
