CFSS Budget Co-Pilot

Explore how planned support-worker costs, approved goods and other expenses could fit inside your existing authorized CFSS service budget. Use figures from your approved service delivery plan and your FMS spending reports.

Not an official DHS or FMS system.This tool does not establish eligibility, calculate a DHS authorization, approve purchases, process payroll or replace your consultation services provider, lead agency, case manager or financial management services (FMS) provider. All costs are user-entered assumptions. Do not enter names, case numbers, SSNs, medical information, or other sensitive information.

🔒 No accounts or uploads

Calculations and FMS report comparisons run in your browser without third-party analytics or uploading the figures you enter. Normal website hosting access logs may still record page visits. Refreshing discards entries unless you choose to download a private JSON plan.

📋 Confirm official numbers

Use your approved service delivery plan and the latest FMS monthly spending report as the source of truth. Budget spending rules, allowable purchases and costs require consultation/FMS review. A projected affordable schedule is never an approved service authorization.

🧮 Estimates, not authorization

Monthly worker cost uses 52 weeks divided by 12 months. It assumes a constant schedule and user-supplied employer burden, not DHS rate formulas.

Source references: Minnesota DHS CFSS FAQ · Minn. Stat. §256B.85. Review current DHS/FMS guidance before making financial commitments.
