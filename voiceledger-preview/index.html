How it works

  1. Tap START SPEAKING in a supported HTTPS browser, or choose iPhone Keyboard Mic.
  2. Speak at your own pace. Pauses do not calculate anything. Tap Done Speaking — Review only when completely finished.
  3. Confirm the displayed details and tap Save reviewed entry. Or choose Edit details. Nothing saves just because the microphone heard you.
iPhone tip

If browser microphone listening will not start, choose “Use iPhone Keyboard Mic,” then tap the microphone on your iPhone keyboard. Apple Dictation can enter your words in the box.

Checking microphone options…

Microphone blocked by your browser

VoiceLedger cannot change Safari permissions for you. Here are two ways to speak without typing:

  1. Use Apple Dictation right now: tap Use iPhone Keyboard Mic, then tap the microphone on your iPhone keyboard. If it is missing: Settings → General → Keyboard → Enable Dictation.
  2. To try the large browser mic again in Safari: open the site in Safari, use the Page Menu → Website Settings → Microphone → Allow (if shown). Or open iPhone Settings → Apps → Safari → Microphone → Ask or Allow. Return here and tap Try browser mic again.

A permitted microphone does not guarantee browser speech recognition. Some browsers still block this feature. This check does not record or save your audio.

Export for your accountant

Offline backup vault

A JSON backup contains your income, expenses, mileage, invoice data, and settings. Save it somewhere private; it may contain customer details.

Warning: backups contain business and customer information. Do not put them in public messages, shared notes, or untrusted apps. Keep a private copy.

Recommended: back up every Friday and before changing phones or clearing browser data. Restoring replaces the current ledger after confirmation.

Vehicle & meal expense protection

Vehicle expenses categorized as Vehicle costs (non-deductible with mileage) still reduce cash profit, but are excluded from this simplified mileage-based tax estimate. Business meals default to a 50% estimate when flagged deductible.

Check with your tax professional before applying a deduction or using the standard mileage method. A business may be ineligible or face other limitations.

Quick-start guide

  1. Open this voiceledger-pro.html file in a modern browser on your device.
  2. Enter your business name and select a reserve percentage in Settings.
  3. Add collected income, paid expenses, and business mileage manually, or try the voice button.
  4. Use Invoices & Receipts to make printable billing documents.
  5. Visit Tax & Exports to download your CSV records for accountant review.
  6. Use Offline Backup (.JSON) weekly and store it securely. Restore only from a backup you trust.

Optional practice data

Practice records are clearly labeled DEMO and excluded from your real financial totals and accountant CSV exports. Do not mark them as real transactions.

Reset local records

To erase all entries, trips, invoices, and settings on this device, use the reset control. This cannot be undone without a JSON backup.
