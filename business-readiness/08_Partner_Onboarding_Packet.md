# Partner Onboarding Packet

## Purpose
Provide a complete onboarding package for clinics, dialysis centers, assisted living facilities, and healthcare coordinators.

## Owner
Partnership Director (primary), Operations Owner (workflow readiness), Security/Compliance Lead (policy section).

## When Used
Immediately after partnership intent and before pilot go-live.

## Draft Packet Content

### 1. Welcome and Program Summary
- Mission and operating model of Amicor Health ISF
- What services are included in the pilot
- Key outcomes expected for partner and riders

### 2. Partner Readiness Requirements
- Signed Provider Service Agreement
- Named operational contacts (primary/backup)
- Authorized requestor list
- Service locations and hours confirmed

### 3. Workflow Overview
- Client intake workflow
- Ride request submission workflow
- Assignment and dispatch communications
- Exception handling workflow
- Weekly reporting and invoice workflow

### 4. Roles and Responsibilities Matrix
| Function | Partner | Amicor |
|---|---|---|
| Request submission quality | Primary | Support |
| Dispatch coordination | Notify/escalate | Primary |
| Rider communication | Primary | Support per policy |
| Billing reconciliation | Joint | Joint |

### 5. Training Plan
- Session 1: Partner portal and request standards
- Session 2: Exception handling and escalation
- Session 3: Reporting, invoicing, and KPI review
- Completion sign-off required before go-live

### 6. Support and Escalation Contacts
- Day-to-day operations contact: [Name/phone/email]
- Escalation manager: [Name/phone/email]
- Incident hotline/process: [Insert]

### 7. Data Security and Privacy Summary
- Minimum necessary data sharing principles
- Access control approach
- Incident reporting commitment

### 8. Pilot Go-Live Checklist
- Test ride completed in staging/controlled mode
- Contacts confirmed and reachable
- Communication templates approved
- Initial reporting schedule agreed

### 9. Appendix
- SLA
- Cancellation Policy
- No-Show Policy
- Escalation Policy
- Rider Service Policy

## Missing Information Required from Amicor
- Final partner onboarding training materials/slides
- Named operations and escalation contacts
- Final partner portal support model (hours/channels)
- Segment-specific onboarding variants (clinic vs dialysis vs assisted living)
