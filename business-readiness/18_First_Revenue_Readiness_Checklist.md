# First Revenue Readiness Checklist

## Purpose
Ensure all prerequisites are in place to generate, send, and collect the first invoice and convert to recurring revenue.

## Owner
Finance Owner (primary), COO (approval), Partnership Director (contract conversion).

## When Used
Before first invoice cycle, during first billing close, and before recurring contract conversion.

## Draft Checklist Content

### 1. Commercial Preconditions
- Pricing terms signed in provider agreement
- Billing contacts and payment terms confirmed
- Dispute process communicated to partner

### 2. Operational Preconditions
- Completed ride records are auditable
- Cancellation and no-show handling is documented per policy
- Exception outcomes are resolved before billing close

### 3. Billing Preconditions
- Invoice template approved
- Weekly billing cut-off time defined
- Billing owner and backup owner assigned

### 4. Payout Preconditions
- Driver payout rules finalized
- Payout approval workflow documented
- Settlement timing and owner confirmed

### 5. Reconciliation Controls
- Invoice and payout reconciliation checklist completed
- Variance review process defined
- Approval matrix for adjustments documented

### 6. Collection and Cash Controls
- Invoice delivery confirmation process in place
- Accounts receivable follow-up cadence defined
- Late payment escalation path defined

### 7. Recurring Revenue Conversion
- Pilot outcome review completed with partner
- Recurring scope and volume commitments negotiated
- Renewal or recurring agreement drafted/signed

### 8. Evidence Archive
- First invoice copy archived
- Payment confirmation archived
- First revenue milestone report completed

## Missing Information Required from Amicor
- Invoice and payout system-of-record ownership
- Collection workflow and escalation timelines
- Revenue recognition policy for pilot services
- Standard recurring contract term and renewal process
