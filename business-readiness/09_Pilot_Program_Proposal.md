# Pilot Program Proposal

## Purpose
Present a structured pilot offer to prospective healthcare partners, aligned to measurable operational and access outcomes.

## Owner
Partnership Director (primary), COO (commercial approval), Operations Owner (delivery plan).

## When Used
During proposal stage after discovery calls and before contract signature.

## Final Pilot Commercial Proposal

### 1. Executive Summary
Amicor Health ISF proposes a 6-week transportation coordination pilot for a dialysis-center partner to improve appointment access, reduce missed visits, and create auditable operational outcomes.

### 2. Pilot Objectives
- Improve ride completion reliability for target population
- Reduce missed/late appointments tied to transportation barriers
- Establish repeatable operational and reporting workflows
- Validate financial model for recurring service

### 3. Pilot Scope
- Partner sites: up to 2 dialysis sites within the launch corridor
- Target rider groups: recurring ambulatory dialysis riders
- Service hours: Monday-Saturday, 5:00 AM-7:00 PM
- Estimated weekly ride volume: 40-70 one-way trips

### 4. Operating Model
- Request intake via authorized partner coordinators
- Manual dispatch control with monitored exception handling
- Driver assignment and lifecycle status updates
- Weekly reporting + billing cadence

### 5. Success Metrics
| KPI | Baseline | Pilot Target |
|---|---|---|
| Completion rate | 85% | >=95% |
| Cancellation rate | 12% | <=8% |
| Exception resolution time | 45 minutes | <=20 minutes |
| On-time pickup rate | 82% | >=92% |

### 6. Pilot Timeline
- Week 0: Contracting + onboarding
- Week 1: Training + controlled launch
- Weeks 2-5: Full pilot operations
- Week 6: Results review + expansion decision

### 7. Partner Commitments
- Appoint primary and backup coordinators
- Provide authorized requestor list
- Participate in weekly operations review
- Support rider readiness communication

### 8. Amicor Commitments
- Deliver defined SLA-backed operations
- Provide escalation coverage and incident management
- Deliver weekly KPI and operational reports
- Provide invoice and reconciliation transparency

### 9. Commercial Terms
- Pilot fee model: flat-rate per completed one-way trip
- Rate: USD 68 per completed one-way trip
- Weekly minimum invoice floor: USD 2,000 (waived for first 2 pilot weeks)
- Billing cadence: weekly
- Payment terms: Net 15

### 10. Expansion Path
If pilot targets are met, transition to recurring program with:
- Expanded service windows and rider volume
- Contracted monthly service terms
- Multi-site rollout eligible after two consecutive target-meeting pilot weeks

## Missing Information Required from Amicor
- Named partner account and contracting contact details
- Confirmed pilot start date and kickoff calendar
