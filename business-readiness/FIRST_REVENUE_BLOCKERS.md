## Minimum Blocker Set Required To Legally And Operationally Sign First Pilot

Minimum must-close set (from already identified blockers):
1. Provider agreement legal identity not finalized
2. Insurance and certificate details unresolved in provider agreement
3. Missing formal HIPAA/legal posture
4. Driver credential governance unresolved (background checks/permits)
5. Pilot proposal missing partner-specific site names and kickoff date
6. KPI source-of-truth schema not locked
7. AR owner and collections workflow not operationally locked
8. Manual dispatch staffing proof not defined for upper-volume scenario
9. Communication failover matrix undefined
10. Data retention/deletion and vendor inventory not finalized

Why this is the minimum set:
- It covers signature legality (contracting identity, insurance, healthcare compliance, defined pilot scope).
- It covers operational signability (credentialed workforce control, KPI governance, staffing proof, communication failover, AR execution readiness).
- All items are already identified as Critical/High blockers in existing documents.
