# Escalation Policy

## Purpose
Define incident severity levels, escalation paths, and response time requirements for ride operations.

## Owner
COO (policy owner), Operations Owner (execution), Partnership Director (partner communications).

## When Used
For ride exceptions, safety incidents, service interruptions, and repeated SLA breaches.

## Draft Policy Content

### 1. Escalation Severity Levels
| Severity | Description | Examples | Initial Response Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sev 1 Critical | Immediate rider safety or active service outage | Safety threat, major system outage, lost rider contact in transit | <= 5 min |
| Sev 2 High | Significant service disruption | No available driver for urgent appointment, repeated failed assignment | <= 15 min |
| Sev 3 Medium | Single-ride exception with manageable impact | Late pickup risk, communication failure | <= 30 min |
| Sev 4 Low | Administrative or non-urgent issue | Reporting discrepancy, minor workflow delay | <= 1 business day |

### 2. Escalation Chain
1. Dispatcher/Operations Coordinator
2. Operations Supervisor
3. On-Call Manager
4. COO or Executive Incident Lead
5. Partner Executive Contact (if contractual threshold met)

### 3. Incident Handling Requirements
- Open incident record with timestamp and severity
- Assign accountable incident owner
- Send partner notification at trigger thresholds
- Document mitigation, decision log, and closure outcome

### 4. Communication Rules
- Internal updates every [15/30/60] minutes for Sev 1/2
- Partner updates at incident open and key status changes
- Closure summary delivered within [24] hours for Sev 1/2

### 5. Post-Incident Review
Required for Sev 1 and recurring Sev 2 incidents:
- Root cause analysis
- Corrective actions with owners and deadlines
- Policy, training, or process updates as needed

### 6. SLA Integration
Escalation events are tracked against SLA commitments and included in weekly operational reports.
