# Grant Submission Calendar

## Purpose
Establish a repeatable grant pipeline timeline with owners, deadlines, and evidence readiness checkpoints.

## Owner
Grant Director (primary), COO (executive sign-off), Ops and Partnerships (evidence contributors).

## When Used
Weekly grant operations review and pre-submission governance.

## Draft Calendar Content

| Cycle | Target Submission Month | Grant Theme | Priority | Owner | Status |

### Per-Submission Timeline (8-Week Backward Plan)
- T-8 weeks: Opportunity qualification and go/no-go
- T-7 weeks: Owner matrix and workplan finalized
- T-6 weeks: Narrative draft v1 complete
- T-5 weeks: Budget draft and pilot plan draft complete
- T-4 weeks: Evidence package draft (screenshots/letters/KPI exports)
- T-3 weeks: Internal review and revisions
- T-2 weeks: Executive/legal/compliance review
- T-1 week: Final quality check and submission packet freeze
- T: Submission completed and logged
- T+1 week: Lessons learned and follow-up plan

### Governance Checkpoints
- Weekly grant standup
- Biweekly narrative and evidence quality review
- Executive approval checkpoint at T-2 weeks

## Missing Information Required from Amicor
