# Budget Workbook Structure

## Purpose
Define a standard workbook layout for grant applications, pilot planning, and launch-stage financial governance.

## Owner
Finance Owner (primary), Grant Director (submission alignment), COO (approval).

## When Used
Grant submissions, pilot proposal costing, recurring service pricing review.

## Draft Workbook Structure

### Workbook Tabs
1. Assumptions
2. Personnel and Contractor Costs
3. Technology and Infrastructure Costs
4. Pilot Operations Costs
5. Compliance and Risk Costs
6. Revenue Assumptions
7. Margin Analysis
8. Cash Flow Forecast
9. Grant Budget Narrative Mapping
10. Scenario Analysis (Base/Upside/Stress)

### Tab 1: Assumptions
- Service area and volume assumptions
- Ride mix assumptions
- Driver payout assumptions
- SLA performance assumptions

### Tab 2: Personnel and Contractor Costs
- Operations staffing
- Partnership and grants staffing
- Driver payout pool assumptions

### Tab 3: Technology and Infrastructure
- Hosting/compute/storage
- Monitoring and support tooling
- Communication tooling (SMS/email)

### Tab 4: Pilot Operations
- Onboarding and training costs
- Incident response and QA costs
- Partner support costs

### Tab 5: Compliance and Risk
- Legal review costs
- Insurance costs
- Security/compliance overhead

### Tab 6: Revenue Assumptions
- Price per completed ride
- Minimum commitments
- Cancellation/no-show fee assumptions

### Tab 7: Margin Analysis
- Gross revenue
- Driver payout costs
- Operating costs
- Contribution margin and margin percent

### Tab 8: Cash Flow Forecast
- Weekly invoice generation
- Collection timing assumptions
- Payout timing assumptions

### Tab 9: Grant Mapping
- Budget line to narrative objective mapping
- Restricted vs unrestricted funds assumptions

### Tab 10: Scenarios
- Base case
- Upside case
- Stress case

## Missing Information Required from Amicor
- Current actual cost baselines
- Target margin policy and break-even thresholds
- Partner segment-specific volume expectations
- Grant-specific allowability constraints
