# No-Show Policy

## Purpose
Define how no-show events are determined, documented, and resolved to ensure fairness and operational consistency.

## Owner
Operations Owner (primary), Finance Owner (billing effects), Driver Network Owner (driver-side consistency).

## When Used
During active rides, weekly invoice/payout review, and dispute resolution.

## Final Policy Content

### 1. No-Show Definitions
- Rider no-show: rider unavailable after required wait period at pickup.
- Driver no-show: assigned driver does not arrive or fails to update status.
- Provider no-show communication failure: provider unavailable for required exception decisions.

### 2. Required Wait Windows
- Driver must wait at pickup for at least 15 minutes unless safety issue exists.
- Driver must attempt contact via approved channels during wait window.
- Driver must log arrival and wait timestamps.

### 3. Rider No-Show Procedure
1. Driver marks arrived status.
2. Driver initiates contact attempts.
3. Driver waits required window.
4. Driver marks no-show exception with evidence.
5. Dispatch confirms and closes or reassigns per provider instruction.

### 4. Driver No-Show Procedure
1. Dispatch monitors assignment response and en-route updates.
2. If no progress in threshold window, dispatch escalates and contacts driver.
3. Ride is reassigned or cancelled based on urgency and availability.
4. Driver reliability event recorded for performance review.

### 5. Billing and Payout Handling
- Rider no-show: charge USD 41 (60 percent of flat trip rate); driver payout USD 13 when evidence is valid
- Driver no-show: no payout for missed assignment; possible penalty rules if contracted
- Documented exception required for all financial outcomes

### 6. Dispute Process
- Disputes must be submitted within 7 business days
- Required evidence: status log, communication attempts, timestamps
- Resolution owner: Finance + Operations

## Missing Information Required from Amicor
- Evidence minimum standard for valid no-show claim
- Driver reliability threshold and corrective action framework:
	- 2 driver no-show events in rolling 30 days: 7-day suspension review
	- 3 driver no-show events in rolling 30 days: contract termination review
