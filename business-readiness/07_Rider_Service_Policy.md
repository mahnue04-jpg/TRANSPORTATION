# Rider Service Policy

## Purpose
Establish rider service standards for safe, consistent, and respectful transportation coordination.

## Owner
Operations Owner (primary), Compliance Lead (review), Driver Network Owner (driver training alignment).

## When Used
During partner onboarding, driver onboarding, and active operations quality management.

## Draft Policy Content

### 1. Service Principles
- Safety first
- Dignity and respect
- Equity and accessibility
- Reliable communication
- Documented accountability

### 2. Eligibility and Request Rules
- Rides are requested by authorized provider/partner staff
- Required trip data must be complete before scheduling
- Service area and operating window rules apply

### 3. Accessibility and Accommodation
- Accessibility needs must be captured at intake
- Vehicle capability must match rider requirements
- Reasonable accommodation process defined for special needs

### 4. Rider Interaction Standards
Drivers and staff must:
- Communicate professionally
- Confirm rider identity safely
- Follow privacy and confidentiality rules
- Avoid discrimination or harassment

### 5. Safety and Conduct Rules
- Zero tolerance for unsafe operation or misconduct
- Mandatory incident reporting for safety concerns
- Emergency escalation follows Escalation Policy

### 6. Communication Expectations
- Provider receives updates at key milestones
- Exception alerts are sent promptly
- Ride completion confirmation is sent with timestamp

### 7. Service Exceptions
Common exceptions:
- Rider no-show
- Driver delay/no-show
- Weather/safety disruption
- Wrong address or readiness issue

Resolution follows No-Show, Cancellation, and Escalation policies.

### 8. Complaints and Feedback
- Complaint intake channel: [Email/portal/phone]
- Acknowledgment target: [1 business day]
- Resolution target: [5 business days]
