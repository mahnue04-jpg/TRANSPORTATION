# Independent Contractor Driver Agreement

## Purpose
Set legal and operational terms for vetted independent drivers delivering rides under Amicor Health ISF operations.

## Owner
Driver Network Owner (primary), Legal/Compliance Lead (review), COO (approval).

## When Used
Before driver activation, before first dispatch assignment, and during annual re-attestation.

## Final Commercial and Operational Terms

### 1. Parties
This Independent Contractor Agreement is entered into by and between:
- Contracting Entity: Amicor Health ISF ("Amicor")
- Contractor: driver identified in signature block
- Effective Date: date of last signature

### 2. Contractor Relationship
- Driver is an independent contractor, not an employee.
- Driver controls means/method of work within service and safety standards.
- No benefits, tax withholding, or employment guarantees are provided.

### 3. Scope of Services
Driver agrees to:
- Accept eligible ride assignments through approved channels
- Perform safe, professional rider transportation
- Maintain timely status updates (assigned, en route, pickup, dropoff)
- Report incidents, delays, and exceptions promptly

### 4. Eligibility and Documentation Requirements
Driver must maintain at all times:
- Valid driver's license
- Vehicle registration
- Active auto insurance meeting required limits
- Background check clearance
- Any local/regional transportation permits

### 5. Service Standards
- On-time pickup expectations: 92 percent minimum monthly performance
- Rider interaction standards: respectful, non-discriminatory, professional
- Communication standards: respond to dispatch notifications within 5 minutes
- Safety compliance: no impaired operation, no unsafe conduct

### 6. Compensation and Payout
- Compensation model: flat-rate per completed one-way ambulatory trip
- Payout cycle: weekly
- Payout method: ACH
- Base payout: USD 38 per completed one-way trip
- Peak payout (5:00-7:00 AM and 4:00-7:00 PM): USD 42 per completed one-way trip
- Rider no-show payout: USD 13 with validated evidence
- Same-day cancellation payout after acceptance: USD 10
- Active-trip cancellation payout after en_route_pickup: USD 24
- Deductions/withholds: permitted only where required by law or fraud/error correction supported by audit record
- Dispute process for payout review: written dispute within 7 business days; resolution within 5 business days

### 7. Assignment Acceptance and Cancellations
- Acceptance/decline response window: 5 minutes
- Driver cancellation rules: allowed for safety risk, verified vehicle issue, or medical emergency; driver must notify dispatch immediately
- Repeated late cancellation thresholds and consequences: 2 late cancellations in rolling 30 days triggers performance review; 3 triggers suspension review

### 8. Confidentiality and Privacy
- Driver may access limited rider data only as required for trip completion
- No unauthorized sharing, recording, or retention of rider information
- Any suspected privacy incident must be reported within 4 hours

### 9. Technology and Reporting
- Driver must use required app/communication channels when on assignment
- Driver must keep contact details current
- Driver must maintain data accuracy for trip status and completion records

### 10. Suspension and Termination
Amicor may suspend or terminate for:
- Missing/expired compliance documentation
- Safety incidents or policy violations
- Fraudulent reporting or misuse of rider/provider information
- Repeated reliability failures per policy thresholds

### 11. Indemnification and Liability
- Contractor is responsible for own acts/omissions and vehicle operation risks
- Contractor maintains required insurance coverage and proof on request

### 12. Acknowledgment and Signature
Driver acknowledges they have read and accept:
- Rider Service Policy
- No-Show Policy
- Cancellation Policy
- Escalation Policy

Signature block:
- Driver Name / Signature / Date
- Amicor Representative / Signature / Date

## Missing Information Required from Amicor
- Insurance minimum coverage thresholds
- Background check provider/process details
- Local licensing/permit requirements by service area
- Suspension/termination appeal process owner
