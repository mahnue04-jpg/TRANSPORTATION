# EXECUTION BOARD

## Objective
Close only the gaps that directly unlock:
1. First pilot partner
2. First recurring rider
3. First invoice
4. First revenue
5. First grant submission

## WEEK 1
### Outcome Targets
- Contract-ready commercial package
- Outreach-ready partner package

### Critical Activities
1. Finalize legal and pricing terms in 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 12.
2. Approve governing law, liability, insurance, breach notification, and dispute terms in 01 and 11.
3. Lock final SLA targets and KPI definitions for partner-facing commitments in 03 and 16.
4. Publish named on-call roster and escalation contacts in 06 and 08.
5. Complete contact and messaging fields in 10.

### Milestone Link
- Directly enables first pilot partner contracting.

### Owners
- Legal Lead, Finance Lead, COO, Operations Lead, Partnership Director, Compliance Lead.

## WEEK 2
### Outcome Targets
- Signed pilot partner
- Operationally launchable pilot setup

### Critical Activities
1. Build partner-specific pilot proposal with hard numbers in 09.
2. Execute partner onboarding packet with segment-specific details in 08.
3. Confirm authorized requestors, service hours, escalation contacts, and recurring schedule candidates.
4. Complete pilot go/no-go authority matrix and criteria in 17.
5. Finalize driver activation standards and compliance checks from 02 and 07.

### Milestone Link
- Directly enables first pilot partner and first recurring rider schedule.

### Owners
- Partnership Director, Operations Lead, Driver Ops Lead, COO, Compliance Lead.

## WEEK 3
### Outcome Targets
- First recurring rider flow operational
- First invoice issued

### Critical Activities
1. Run pilot rides with recurring rider cohort and enforce 04/05/06 policies.
2. Capture auditable KPI baseline and pilot results using finalized definitions from 16.
3. Close first weekly billing cycle and issue first invoice (18).
4. Execute payout approvals and reconciliation controls from 18.
5. Start AR follow-up cadence and dispute workflow.

### Milestone Link
- Directly enables first recurring rider, first invoice, and path to first revenue.

### Owners
- Operations Lead, Finance Lead, COO, Partnership Director.

## WEEK 4
### Outcome Targets
- First cash collection (first revenue)
- First complete grant submission packet

### Critical Activities
1. Convert first invoice to payment using AR escalation process in 18.
2. Finalize grant targets, deadlines, and owner matrix in 13.
3. Assemble complete evidence repository per 14: partner letter(s), KPI exports, policy pack, security one-pager, budget workbook.
4. Populate budget workbook with real pilot and cost baseline data in 15.
5. Submit first grant application and archive confirmation.

### Milestone Link
- Directly enables first revenue and first grant submission.

### Owners
- Finance Lead, Grant Director, Partnership Director, Operations Lead, COO.

## Dependency Order (Do Not Break)
1. Legal/pricing finalization (Week 1) -> 2) partner signature (Week 2) -> 3) recurring rides + invoicing (Week 3) -> 4) cash + grant submission (Week 4).

## Weekly Governance Rhythm
1. Monday: blocker review and owner assignments.
2. Wednesday: evidence check against critical milestones.
3. Friday: milestone gate decision (pass/fail) with COO approval.

## Gate Criteria by Milestone
### Gate A: First Pilot Partner
- Signed provider agreement
- Final pricing + SLA accepted
- Named partner operational contacts confirmed

### Gate B: First Recurring Rider
- Recurring schedule roster active
- Driver cohort active and compliant
- Escalation/on-call workflow tested

### Gate C: First Invoice
- Completed rides auditable
- Billing cut-off and reconciliation complete
- Invoice sent and receipt confirmed

### Gate D: First Revenue
- Payment received and logged
- Disputes resolved or in policy SLA

### Gate E: First Grant Submission
- Calendar and target opportunity confirmed
- Evidence checklist complete
- Budget and KPI exports finalized
- Submission confirmation archived
