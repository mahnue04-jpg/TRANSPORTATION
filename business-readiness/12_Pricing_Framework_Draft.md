# Pricing Framework (Owner Decision)

## Purpose
Define finalized pricing logic for pilots and recurring service, including billing units, safeguards, and margin discipline.

## Owner
Finance Owner (primary), COO (commercial approval), Partnership Director (market fit feedback).

## When Used
Provider contract negotiation, pilot proposal creation, invoice setup, and margin review.

## Finalized Framework Content

### 1. Pricing Objectives
- Achieve sustainable unit economics at pilot scale
- Keep partner pricing transparent and predictable
- Align charges with service levels and complexity

### 2. Selected Pricing Model (Fixed)
Model: Flat-rate per completed one-way trip (B2B healthcare facility contracts)
- Segment: Dialysis centers
- Launch geography: small city / suburban healthcare corridor
- Billing unit: one completed one-way ambulatory medical transportation trip

### 3. Pilot Commercial Terms (Approved)
- Pilot setup fee: USD 0
- Flat rate per completed one-way trip: USD 68
- Same-day cancellation fee (<=2 hours before pickup): USD 34 (50 percent of trip rate)
- Rider no-show fee: USD 41 (60 percent of trip rate)
- Late cancellation fee (>2 hours and <=24 hours): USD 24 (35 percent of trip rate)
- Accessibility/extended service adjustment: USD 0 for launch phase (single ambulatory service class)

### 4. Driver Compensation Framework (Approved)
- Base payout per completed one-way trip: USD 38
- Peak window payout (5:00-7:00 AM and 4:00-7:00 PM): USD 42
- Rider no-show payout (evidence-validated): USD 13
- Same-day cancellation payout after driver acceptance: USD 10
- Active-trip cancellation payout after driver marked en_route_pickup: USD 24
- No distance/time multipliers for launch phase

### 5. Margin Governance
For each billing period track:
- Gross billed amount
- Driver payout amount
- Net margin
- Exception adjustment impact

Minimum margin threshold: 25 percent contribution margin after direct driver payout.
If margin is below threshold for 2 consecutive invoice weeks, trigger pricing review and concession freeze.

### 6. Billing and Payment Terms
- Invoice frequency: weekly
- Payment terms: Net 15
- Dispute window: 7 business days from invoice receipt
- Late payment handling: 1.5 percent monthly service charge on overdue balances after 10-day grace period

### 7. Discount and Concession Guardrails
- Discounts require COO/Finance approval
- Time-limited pilot concessions only
- No uncapped custom terms without written approval
- Maximum discount: 10 percent
- Maximum concession period: first 8 weeks only

## Missing Information Required from Amicor
- Legal entity tax details for invoice header and remittance
- Approved customer payment channels (ACH, check, card)
- Final AR escalation contact list
