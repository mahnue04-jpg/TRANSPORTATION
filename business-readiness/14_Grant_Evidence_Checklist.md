# Grant Evidence Checklist

## Purpose
Standardize all proof materials required to support grant credibility, measurable impact claims, and execution readiness.

## Owner
Grant Director (primary), Operations Owner (KPI evidence), Partnership Director (letters and partner proof).

## When Used
During each grant cycle at draft, review, and pre-submission freeze stages.

## Draft Checklist Content

### A. Narrative Evidence
- Mission/problem statement aligned to grant theme
- Target population and geography definition
- Program model and workflow explanation
- Sustainability plan beyond grant period

### B. Operational Evidence
- Ride lifecycle workflow diagrams
- Dispatch and exception handling process maps
- Pilot readiness checklist completion proof
- Escalation and policy documents

### C. Performance Evidence
- KPI baseline table
- Pilot period KPI snapshots (if available)
- Weekly and monthly trend summaries
- Exception categories and resolution outcomes

### D. Partnership Evidence
- Partner letters of intent or support
- Signed partner agreements (if available)
- Partner onboarding and training completion records

### E. Financial Evidence
- Budget workbook and assumptions
- Pricing framework summary
- Cost model and unit economics rationale
- Invoice/payout governance controls

### F. Security and Privacy Evidence
- Security and privacy one-pager
- Access control and tenant isolation summary
- Incident policy and audit logging summary

### G. Visual Assets
- Product screenshots (intake, dispatch, reports)
- Dashboard exports and report samples
- Pilot timeline and milestone charts

### H. Submission Controls
- Version-controlled submission folder
- Owner sign-off checklist
- Final QA pass completed
- Submission confirmation archived

## Missing Information Required from Amicor
- Named storage location and taxonomy for evidence files
- Required artifact format standards (PDF, PNG, CSV)
- Approval chain for final evidence sign-off
- Current availability of partner letters and pilot KPI exports
