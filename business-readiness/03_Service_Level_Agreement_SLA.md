# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## Purpose
Define measurable service performance targets and accountability rules between Amicor and partner organizations.

## Owner
COO (primary), Operations Owner (metrics), Partnership Director (partner sign-off).

## When Used
Attached to provider agreements, pilot proposals, and quarterly partner performance reviews.

## Draft SLA Content

### 1. Scope
This SLA applies to Health ISF transportation coordination services for approved partner organizations and service areas.

### 2. Service Hours
- Standard service window: [Days/Times]
- Dispatch support window: [Days/Times]
- Emergency escalation support: [Days/Times]

### 3. SLA Metrics and Targets
| Metric | Definition | Target | Measurement Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Request validation turnaround | Time from request submission to valid queued/rejected status | <= [15/30] min | Daily/weekly |
| Assignment turnaround | Time from queued to assigned | <= [60/120] min | Daily/weekly |
| Pickup reliability | % rides with pickup within allowed window | >= [90/95]% | Weekly |
| Completion rate | % non-cancelled rides completed | >= [92/95]% | Weekly |
| Exception response time | Time to first ops response on exception rides | <= [15/30] min | Per incident |
| Provider notification timeliness | Notification sent at key state transitions | >= [98]% success | Weekly |
| Weekly report delivery | Delivery of weekly operating report | 100% by [Day/Time] | Weekly |

### 4. Measurement and Reporting
- Data source: Amicor operational logs and ride lifecycle history
- Reporting cadence: weekly summary plus monthly trend view
- Access method: [Email export/portal download]

### 5. Exclusions
SLA does not apply when impacted by:
- Force majeure events
- Provider-requested schedule constraints outside policy
- Incomplete or invalid ride request data
- Confirmed network/platform outages outside Amicor control

### 6. Escalation and Service Credits
- Breach threshold: [Two consecutive weeks / X incidents]
- Escalation steps: per Escalation Policy
- Service credits or remedies: [Define if offered]

### 7. Review and Revision
- SLA review cadence: quarterly
- Amendments require written approval by both parties

## Missing Information Required from Amicor
- Final target values for each KPI
- Approved measurement definitions and data logic
- Service credit policy (if any)
- Reporting format and owner
- Partner-specific SLA tiers (standard vs pilot vs enterprise)
