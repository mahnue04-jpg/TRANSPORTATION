# Pilot Readiness Checklist

## Purpose
Provide a go/no-go control checklist to confirm legal, operational, partner, and reporting readiness before first live pilot rides.

## Owner
COO (final go/no-go), Operations Owner (execution), Partnership Director (partner readiness).

## When Used
Final week before pilot launch and day-of-launch readiness check.

## Draft Checklist Content

### A. Legal and Contracting
- Provider Service Agreement signed
- Driver contractor agreements signed
- SLA, Cancellation, No-Show, Escalation, Rider policies approved

### B. Partner Readiness
- Partner primary/backup contacts confirmed
- Authorized requestor list confirmed
- Partner onboarding training completed
- Pilot scope and service hours confirmed

### C. Driver Readiness
- Driver documents verified and current
- Driver cohort activated for pilot windows
- Driver communication and escalation process tested

### D. Operations Readiness
- Dispatch workflow dry run completed
- Exception handling drill completed
- Escalation chain tested
- On-call roster published

### E. Technical and Reporting Readiness
- Health checks passing
- Core ride lifecycle path validated
- KPI dashboard/report exports available
- Weekly report template ready

### F. Financial Readiness
- Pricing and billing rules finalized
- Invoice template approved
- Payout batch process defined
- Reconciliation owner assigned

### G. Launch Governance
- Risk register reviewed
- Go/no-go meeting completed
- Rollback or pause criteria defined

Go/No-Go Decision:

- Signatories: COO, Ops, Partnerships, Finance, Legal

## Missing Information Required from Amicor
- Named signatories and decision authority matrix
- Final launch date and pilot volume target
- Documented rollback/pause criteria
- Confirmed report owners and publishing cadence
