# Cancellation Policy

## Purpose
Set consistent rules for ride cancellations to protect rider access, operational reliability, and fair billing/payout handling.

## Owner
Operations Owner (primary), Finance Owner (billing impacts), Partnership Director (partner communication).

## When Used
Referenced during ride operations, partner onboarding, invoice review, and dispute handling.

## Final Policy Content

### 1. Cancellation Types
- Provider-initiated cancellation
- Rider-initiated cancellation (reported through provider)
- Driver-initiated cancellation
- Amicor-initiated cancellation (safety, compliance, force majeure)

### 2. Timing Windows
- Standard cancellation: >24 hours before pickup
- Late cancellation: <=24 hours and >2 hours before pickup
- Same-day cancellation: <=2 hours before pickup
- Active-trip cancellation: after driver marked en_route_pickup

### 3. Operational Handling Rules
- All cancellations must include reason code and notes
- Provider contact must be notified for all non-provider cancellations
- Driver reassignment should be attempted before cancellation when feasible

### 4. Billing and Payout Rules
- Standard cancellation: no charge, no payout
- Late cancellation (>2 hours and <=24 hours before pickup): charge USD 24, no driver payout unless already accepted; accepted assignment payout USD 8
- Same-day cancellation (<=2 hours before pickup): charge USD 34, driver payout USD 10 if assignment accepted
- Active-trip cancellation (after driver marked en_route_pickup): charge full trip USD 68, driver payout USD 24

### 5. Documentation Requirements
Each cancellation record must include:
- Timestamp
- Initiator role
- Reason category
- Supporting notes/evidence
- Notification confirmation

### 6. Exception Overrides
Operations supervisor may override policy in cases involving:
- Medical urgency
- Safety risk
- Severe weather/regional emergency
- System outage causing workflow failure

### 7. Communication Standards
- Provider notification target: within 10 minutes of status change
- Rider-facing communication handled via provider unless otherwise agreed

## Missing Information Required from Amicor
- Final reason code taxonomy
- Financial ownership of override approvals
- Whether rider-facing direct communication is in scope
- Jurisdiction-specific legal constraints on cancellation charges
