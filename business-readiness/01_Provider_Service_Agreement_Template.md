# Provider Service Agreement Template

## Purpose
Define the commercial and operational terms between Amicor Health ISF and a healthcare provider organization for transportation coordination services.

## Owner
Partnership Director (primary), Legal/Compliance Lead (review), COO (approval).

## When Used
Before pilot launch, before live ride requests, and before invoice generation.

## Final Business Terms (Owner Approved)

### 1. Parties
This Provider Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between:
- Service Provider: Amicor Health ISF ("Amicor")
- Client Organization: Contracted healthcare facility identified in signature block ("Provider")
- Effective Date: Date of last signature

### 2. Services
Amicor will provide transportation coordination services including:
- Rider intake support through provider-submitted requests
- Ride request validation and dispatch coordination
- Driver assignment and status tracking
- Exception handling and escalation communication
- Weekly reporting and invoice generation

### 3. Service Area and Operating Window
- Approved service area: Small City / Suburban Healthcare Corridor (ZIP list maintained in Exhibit A)
- Standard operating hours: Monday-Saturday, 5:00 AM-7:00 PM local time
- Holiday and after-hours rules: rides outside standard hours are out of scope during pilot unless approved in writing by both parties

### 4. Roles and Responsibilities
Provider responsibilities:
- Submit complete and accurate ride request data
- Maintain authorized requestor list
- Provide rider readiness information and contact details
- Respond to exception notices within SLA windows

Amicor responsibilities:
- Process valid requests according to SLA
- Coordinate driver assignment and status updates
- Notify Provider at key ride lifecycle milestones
- Maintain auditable logs and reporting exports

### 5. Service Level Commitments
Service levels are governed by the signed SLA addendum.
- SLA document reference: business-readiness/03_Service_Level_Agreement_SLA.md
- Escalation policy reference: business-readiness/06_Escalation_Policy.md

### 6. Pricing and Billing
- Pricing schedule reference: business-readiness/12_Pricing_Framework_Draft.md (Owner Decision Version)
- Invoice frequency: Weekly
- Invoice delivery method: PDF plus CSV via designated billing email
- Payment terms: Net 15 days
- Approved billing contacts: designated in onboarding packet and confirmed before first ride week

### 7. Cancellations, No-Shows, and Exceptions
- Governed by Cancellation Policy and No-Show Policy addenda
- Same-day cancellation fee rules: USD 34 when cancelled within 2 hours of pickup
- No-show determination window: 15-minute documented wait at pickup

### 8. Data Security and Privacy
- Minimum safeguards: role-based access, tenant isolation, audit trail logging
- Protected data handling: limited to minimum necessary operations data
- Breach/incident notification window: 48 hours from confirmed security incident affecting Provider data

### 9. Compliance and Audit Rights
- Provider may request service/audit evidence related to billed rides
- Amicor will provide operational logs, status history, and exception records

### 10. Term and Termination
- Initial term: 6-month pilot-to-recurring term
- Renewal: automatic month-to-month after initial term unless terminated
- Termination for cause: 15-day cure period
- Termination for convenience: 30-day written notice

### 11. Liability and Indemnification
- Mutual indemnification applies for third-party claims caused by each party's negligence, misconduct, or breach of law
- Limitation of liability: each party's aggregate liability capped at fees paid or payable in prior 3 months, excluding fraud and willful misconduct
- Insurance requirements: commercial auto coverage minimum 100/300/50 or equivalent, and general liability minimum USD 1,000,000 per occurrence

### 12. Dispute Resolution
- Escalation path before legal action
- Governing law: state where contracted Provider principal service location is registered
- Venue: county of contracted Provider principal service location
